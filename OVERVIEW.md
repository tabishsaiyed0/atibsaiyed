# Atib Portfolio

A public portfolio for Atib, a Website & AI Automation Expert with 5+ years of experience. It's aimed at small business owners (clinics, agencies, coaches, real estate, local services) who want more leads and less busywork.

Visitors see Atib's services, a scrolling gallery of example builds, and a contact form. Every enquiry is saved to the Leads table and emailed to Atib. The projects shown are labelled "Example build" until real case studies replace them.
